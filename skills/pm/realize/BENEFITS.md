# Benefits realization

The gates get an initiative approved and delivered; realize is where the practice finds out whether any of it was actually worth it. Business value delivered is the metric that closes the loop the case opened.

## The benefits register

Every benefit the case projected is entered into the register with its projection, its trace back to the PRD's success metrics and the case's cost-benefit line, and — once the initiative is live — its actuals. A benefit with no trace to where it was originally projected cannot be checked, only asserted.

## Leading indicators

Waiting for the lagging benefit to arrive means finding out too late that a projection was wrong. Every benefit in the register carries at least one leading indicator that moves before the lagging outcome does, so a divergence is visible during delivery rather than only at the retrospective.

## The north star

Every benefit rolls up to the practice's north star — efficiency and productivity as the overall transformation value the practice exists to deliver. An initiative that cannot state its contribution to the north star is a question for the grill, not an entry that gets a pass. This is where costing and benefit meet: the register tracks actuals against `COSTING.md`'s projections on both sides of the ledger, cost and benefit together, so the transformation-value story never separates from the cost story.

## Variance and the loop back

When actuals diverge from projection, the variance is not filed and forgotten. It re-enters `discover` or `carve` as new work, carrying its own case for why closing the gap is worth doing — the same discipline that opened the initiative in the first place, now applied to the gap it left behind.
