# PM group — the AI-native transformation practice

Sixteen promoted skills: the business side of the same AI-native transformation coin as the developer group. The goal, stated and tested: the PM becomes a UX-driven, outcome-driven, efficient agentic owner of problem-solving — reasoning through problems with an agent fleet whose blind spots the PM assesses, and whose coverage closes the PM's own blind spot in return. PMs work one hat at a time, product or transformation, never both — the keepers of the transformation, with visibility into the delivery teams they steer toward a north star of efficiency and productivity as transformation value, every initiative grounded in cost including the agent fleet's own.

Four human gates — Framing, Investment, Quality, Commitment — mirror the developer group's four; approval stays human at every one. See the root README and wiki/Group-PM.md.
