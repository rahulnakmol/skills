# Costing

Every initiative is grounded in cost — not just what it costs to build, but what it costs to run, what it costs to not do something else instead, and what it costs to run the agent fleet the PM commissioned to do the work. A case missing any of these four is not actually costed, it is partially costed.

## Build cost

The one-time cost to design and deliver the initiative: people time, tooling, any external spend. Estimated at the range the team can actually stand behind, not a single false-precision number.

## Run cost

The ongoing cost once the initiative is live: support, maintenance, licensing, and the steady-state agent-fleet spend that continues after delivery, not just during it.

## Agent-fleet cost

The token and run budget for every agent the PM commissions across the initiative's lifecycle — research agents, grill rounds, review panels — stated as an explicit line, not folded silently into build cost. An initiative that never states its agent-fleet cost cannot answer the north star question of whether the agents made the work more efficient or just moved the cost somewhere less visible.

## Opportunity cost

What else that budget and that team's attention could have delivered instead — the cost the do-nothing option in `CASE-METHOD.md` is measured against, and the cost every other candidate initiative in the roadmap is implicitly competing with.

## Actuals

Once the initiative is delivering, `realize` tracks actuals against every projection in this file — build, run, agent-fleet, and the benefit side of the ledger — so the value story and the cost story never separate into two documents that quietly stop agreeing with each other.
