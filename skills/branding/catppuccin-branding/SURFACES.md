# Catppuccin surface direction

## Composition and imagery

Use rounded frames, broad color fields, concise labels, and deliberate asymmetry. Photographs should carry clean subject separation and quiet backgrounds. Avoid coating images with several pastel gradients; use one controlled color relationship around the image instead.

## Product, documents, and data

- Use `focus` blue for interaction and `accent` mauve for identity. Do not swap them because both look expressive.
- Pair every status with a label, icon, shape, or pattern. Neighboring pastel colors alone do not provide dependable distinction.
- Use light mode for printed documents. In decks, use dark mode for section breaks and light mode for dense evidence when both are tested on the room display.
- Use a stable series order. Reserve mauve for the primary series and use line styles or direct labels for comparison.

## Environmental and physical work

Use light neutral walls and place pastel color on bounded planes, furnishings, wayfinding bands, or graphics. Large areas make low-saturation shifts more visible, so approve full-size samples. Record Lab, light reflectance value, substrate, gloss, and lighting. Check that rounded signs and friendly color do not reduce the urgency of safety or regulatory messages.

## Motion and video

Use quick but readable scale, mask, and position changes with a steady cadence. Pastel color can identify chapters, speakers, or data series in titles and graphics. Keep faces and neutral products outside the look. Dark UI captures need tested caption panels rather than a generic shadow. The reduced-motion version uses cuts and opacity changes without scale or simulated depth.
