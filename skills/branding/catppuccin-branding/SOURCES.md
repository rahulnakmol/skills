Sources were checked on September 4, 2026. Record the version or commit used when an artifact enters production.

## Palette

- [Catppuccin palette](https://github.com/catppuccin/palette), MIT license. The source defines Latte, Frappé, Macchiato, and Mocha flavors.

The semantic mapping in `PROFILE.md` is local to this skill. It uses Latte and Mocha as direction and may combine values to meet enhanced contrast. Do not attribute semantic roles, typography, physical specifications, or video guidance to the upstream project.
