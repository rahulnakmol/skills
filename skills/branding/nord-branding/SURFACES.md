# Nord surface direction

## Composition and imagery

Use open grids, low visual noise, aligned edges, thin rules, and larger fields of negative space. Favor images with clean natural light and visible material texture. Avoid cyan shadows, artificial snow effects, or cold casts that make people and products inaccurate.

## Product, documents, and data

- Use Frost blue for focus and the principal series. Use Aurora colors for status only with labels and secondary shapes.
- Keep controls compact but not below the shared target-size rule.
- Use light mode for print. Dark mode works for display and video graphics after testing on the intended screen.
- Use direct labels and line styles so several cool series remain distinguishable.

## Environmental and physical work

Use pale neutral walls, dark wayfinding, and blue detail on bounded surfaces. Test matte paint, frosted film, powder-coated metal, light wood, and textile as separate materials rather than assuming one screen color matches them. Record Lab, light reflectance value, finish, substrate, and intended lighting. Check large pale areas for glare and edge loss.

## Motion and video

Use steady camera movement, clean cuts, directional slides, and controlled depth. Maintain neutral skin and material color; put cool identity into production surfaces, graphics, and selected shadows only after balance. Keep lower thirds open and firmly aligned. The reduced-motion version preserves the sequence through cuts and static position rather than spatial travel.
