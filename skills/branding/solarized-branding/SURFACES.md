# Solarized surface direction

## Composition and imagery

Use disciplined columns, ruled sections, warm open space, and small blue or gold interventions. Images should retain neutral whites and accurate skin. Avoid applying a yellow-and-blue split tone to every photograph.

## Product, documents, and data

- Use blue-green `focus` for interaction and the principal series. Use gold for warning only with a label and shape.
- Use the light and dark modes as tonal inversions of one system, but test each rendered state separately.
- Use light mode on paper. Keep deep blue-green for covers, code blocks, section dividers, and limited display work.
- Use a fixed chart order and direct labels. Do not use all source accents merely because they are available.

## Environmental and physical work

Start with warm neutral walls or surfaces, dark blue-green wayfinding, and limited blue or gold detail. Approve measured samples because warm off-white changes sharply with the light source and surrounding material. Record Lab, light reflectance value, finish, substrate, and intended lighting. Use an actual material standard rather than a screen capture.

## Motion and video

Use clean cuts, measured line draws, panel reveals, and stable type. Keep blue-green and gold in graphics, titles, sets, and controlled shadow detail. Balance footage before applying a look, then protect skin, white objects, safety colors, and evidence-bearing images. The reduced-motion version replaces line draws and panels with static states and direct cuts.
