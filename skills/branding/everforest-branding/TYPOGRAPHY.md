# Everforest typography

Fraunces provides the expressive layer. Its optical sizes and soft forms give large headings an editorial character without relying on decorative illustration. Use it for display text, short pull quotes, numerals, and section openings. Do not use it for dense controls or captions.

Atkinson Hyperlegible Next carries paragraphs, controls, labels, and captions. Its letterforms distinguish commonly confused characters at small sizes. Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono carries code, identifiers, measurements, and production notes.

## Pairing rules

- Use Fraunces at 32 pixels or larger in digital work and 24 points or larger in print.
- Use Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for all interactive text and long reading.
- Keep body lines near 45 to 75 characters. Use a line height from 1.45 to 1.65.
- Use weight, size, and space before changing text color. Both `ink` and `ink-muted` meet the enhanced text target on the canvas.
- Use sentence case. Reserve all capitals for short metadata labels with added letter spacing.
- Use tabular numbers for data tables and dashboards.

## Language support

These primary families do not cover every writing system. Select a Noto Sans or Noto Serif family made for the target script. Match the role and approximate width, then test shaping, diacritics, punctuation, numerals, line breaks, and text expansion with real copy. Package every used font file and its OFL-1.1 license.
