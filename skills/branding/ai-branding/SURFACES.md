# AI branding surface direction

## Composition and imagery

Anthropic uses editorial margins, warm material, compact geometric labels, and selective clay accents. OpenAI uses more open monochrome space, sharper alignment, and restrained teal detail. Both avoid generic glowing brains, circuit heads, robot hands, and decorative code that carries no information.

## Product, documents, and data

- Keep provider variants separate. A component, slide, or chart uses one variant from start to finish.
- Use the display face for structure and the serif face for explanation. Technical products do not require every word to look like code.
- Treat `accent` as identity and `focus` as interaction. Keep provider names out of unlabeled decorative marks.
- Explain generated, inferred, uncertain, or automated content in words. Do not use a sparkle or color alone to disclose it.

## Environmental and physical work

For Anthropic, test warm neutral walls, dark wayfinding, and clay detail in matte material. For OpenAI, test neutral walls, high-contrast black or white structure, and limited teal detail. Measure Lab, light reflectance value, finish, substrate, and intended lighting. A company-inspired profile never turns a provider's screen color into a paint name or grants use of its mark.

## Motion and video

Anthropic favors measured editorial cuts, tactile close views, and warm production light. OpenAI favors clean spatial reveals, controlled monochrome, and precise transitions. Both protect skin, neutral products, source screenshots, and safety colors from the grade. Use provider marks only when supplied with current permission. Never place both providers' marks in one end card unless the content requires factual comparison and the rights and labeling are clear.
