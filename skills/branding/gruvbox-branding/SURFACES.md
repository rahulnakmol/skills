# Gruvbox surface direction

## Composition and imagery

Use dense but ordered columns, large numerals, firm rules, offset blocks, and selective rough texture. Photographs may carry grain when it serves the story, but retain detail in faces, products, diagrams, and evidence. Avoid fake scan damage over text.

## Product, documents, and data

- Use cyan `focus` for interaction and warm `accent` for identity or the primary series.
- Use mono text for data and code, not as a blanket signal that a product is technical.
- Use light mode on paper. Test small reversed text carefully when dark panels are necessary.
- Keep chart axes and labels in `ink`. Use direct labels, patterns, and line styles to separate warm neighboring series.

## Environmental and physical work

Start with warm cream or charcoal fields, dark or light wayfinding, and limited rust accents. Uncoated paper, stained wood, powder-coated metal, and matte paint fit the direction only after measured samples establish contrast. Record Lab, light reflectance value, finish, substrate, and intended light. Distressing is decorative and must never reduce sign or safety-message legibility.

## Motion and video

Use decisive cuts, stepped graphic reveals, mechanical wipes, and restrained frame texture. Keep titles on stable panels rather than vibrating or degrading letterforms. Shape warmth through production light and selective contrast, then protect skin, neutrals, product colors, and source screenshots. A reduced-motion cut replaces wipes and stepped movement with clean cuts and static hierarchy.
