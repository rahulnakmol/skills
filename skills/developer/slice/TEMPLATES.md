# Backlog templates

## Epic
Outcome hypothesis, coordination pod, release train.

## Feature
R-IDs, NFR pointers, tracer bullet scope.

## User story
Vertical slice, acceptance criteria, test notes.

## Technical story
Contracts, migrations, brownfield touch points.

## CI / improvement item
Operability lane — see `OPERABILITY.md`.

## Risk
Statement, owner, likelihood, impact, mitigation, review date. Raised from the pm group's `raid` register; conforms to `WORK-ITEM-CONTRACT.md` like any other item.

## Impediment
What is blocked, who unblocks it, since when, escalation path. Raised from the pm group's `raid` register when an issue crosses from tracked to blocking; conforms to `WORK-ITEM-CONTRACT.md` like any other item.
