Add `exhibit` to the branding group. Exhibit builds one self-contained, interactive HTML page that walks a reader through a topic as a journey of acts, each carrying one claim, one figure, a control that changes a model, and a takeaway that states the result at the reader's settings. The page runs on Alpine.js for state, GSAP with ScrollTrigger for motion, and htmx, pinned by checksum and inlined, and takes its colors, type, motion, and voice from a theme skill's `PROFILE.md` or from a brand directory or `.skill` archive that carries one. A scaffold script writes the shell from that profile and a storyboard, an agent authors the acts from nine playbooks, and a verify script holds the finished page to the storyboard, to the brand's rules including its motion grade, to a rendered capture at desktop and phone widths with a contact sheet, and to a printed PDF with every animation at rest. Three delivery modes, `scroll`, `deck`, and `explorer`, and three registers, `document`, `product`, and `cinematic`, wrap the same act markup; twelve frames show a UI, digital, or print mockup in place. The six theme profiles move to schema version 2 with a `motion` block. Two fixtures exercise the `anthropic` and `openai` variants of `ai-branding`.
