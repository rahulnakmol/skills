## What it does

Everforest Branding applies warm mineral neutrals and forest color to a complete communication system. It uses Fraunces with Atkinson Hyperlegible Next and Mono, all under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The theme supplies light and dark semantic roles, while the shared engine governs language, accessibility, physical sampling, motion, video, and delivery evidence.

## A working example

QuenServe E1 needs a product page, a release PDF, a six-meter event wall, and a 30-second launch video. The skill selects Everforest light, maps the same semantic roles into web and document tokens, and generates a press palette. It provides a physical sampling brief instead of naming a paint from the screen color.

The video brief uses natural production light and keeps skin neutral. Green appears in set details, charts, and titles rather than as a global cast. Captions, a transcript, audio-description need, reduced motion, Rec.709 output, and the encoded-file watch all appear in the review receipt.

## Common questions

<details class="qa"><summary>Does the source palette make an artifact accessible?</summary><div class="qa-body"><p>No. The semantic role validator checks selected pairs. The rendered artifact still needs keyboard, semantics, reflow, forced-color, reduced-motion, and media review.</p></div></details>

<details class="qa"><summary>Can the screen colors specify wall paint?</summary><div class="qa-body"><p>No. The skill requires measured Lab and light reflectance values, substrate and finish records, intended-light review, and approved samples.</p></div></details>

<details class="qa"><summary>Why use light mode for print?</summary><div class="qa-body"><p>It uses less ink and usually reproduces long reading more consistently. A dark digital document remains available when its delivery environment supports it.</p></div></details>

## It's working if

- The meaning and hierarchy survive light, dark, print, forced-color, and reduced-motion states.
- Body text and controls use Atkinson Hyperlegible Next rather than the display face.
- A physical specification cites measured samples, not only screen values.
- The encoded video retains neutral skin, readable captions, correct tags, and the approved message order.

## Where it fits

Everforest Branding is one of six user-invoked themes. It owns the aesthetic choice and then calls the model-invoked branding system. Press remains a separate renderer for approved markdown. A single-theme repository link also brings the required engine, but it does not bring unrelated themes.
