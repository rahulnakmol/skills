## What it does

Catppuccin Branding turns Latte and Mocha direction into an accessible light and dark identity. Syne supplies expressive display type, Nunito Sans handles reading and controls, and JetBrains Mono handles technical material. The shared engine carries one verbal identity and the surface, physical, motion, accessibility, rights, and delivery rules.

## A working example

QuenServe E1 uses dark mode for a product launch page and video graphics, then light mode for the release PDF and print wall brief. Mauve identifies the campaign. Blue retains the focus role. Success, warning, and error states carry text and boundaries, so adjacent pastel colors never carry meaning alone.

The event wall starts as a sampling brief with measured Lab and light reflectance values. The video keeps faces neutral and uses pastel panels for chapters, lower thirds, and chart series. The receipt records captions, transcript, flash test, reduced-motion cut, color tags, and the owner who must approve production.

## Common questions

<details class="qa"><summary>Does this preserve all four Catppuccin flavors?</summary><div class="qa-body"><p>No. Latte and Mocha guide the accessible light and dark pair. A request for another exact flavor must choose between source fidelity and the enhanced semantic contract.</p></div></details>

<details class="qa"><summary>Can pastel colors be used for normal text?</summary><div class="qa-body"><p>Only after the final foreground and background pass the text requirement. The profile reserves dark or light ink roles for ordinary reading.</p></div></details>

<details class="qa"><summary>Can the profile be sent directly to a fabricator?</summary><div class="qa-body"><p>No. Physical production requires measured samples, substrate and finish records, intended-light review, and owner approval.</p></div></details>

## It's working if

- Mauve communicates identity while blue remains a visible focus signal.
- Every status and chart series has a non-color distinction.
- Syne stays at display sizes while Nunito Sans carries controls and long reading.
- Captions and graphics remain readable over every delivered video shot.
