## What it does

Gruvbox Branding turns earthy editor colors into a current cross-surface identity. Bricolage Grotesque carries display character, Newsreader carries long reading, and IBM Plex Mono handles code and data. The theme adds light and dark semantic roles while the shared engine supplies language, accessibility, physical, motion, video, rights, and output contracts.

## A working example

QuenServe E1 needs a dense product status page, an editorial release PDF, a physical event wall, and a short launch video. Gruvbox dark supports the screen and motion graphics. Gruvbox light supports the document and sampling brief. Orange identifies the principal series while cyan remains a focus signal.

The wall uses warm cream, charcoal, and a limited rust accent after full-size measured samples. The edit uses firm cuts and stepped graphic reveals without degraded type. Skin, white products, source screenshots, and safety colors remain protected through the Rec.709 delivery transform.

## Common questions

<details class="qa"><summary>Does the skill include every Gruvbox variant?</summary><div class="qa-body"><p>No. It uses one accessible semantic light and dark pair. An exact hard, soft, bright, neutral, or faded request needs a separate source-fidelity decision.</p></div></details>

<details class="qa"><summary>Can texture cover type or photographs?</summary><div class="qa-body"><p>No. Texture remains decorative. It cannot reduce text contrast, hide evidence, distort a face, or obscure a product or safety message.</p></div></details>

<details class="qa"><summary>Can dark mode be the default printed page?</summary><div class="qa-body"><p>Not by default. The light profile uses less ink and supports long reading. A dark printed object requires an explicit production and proof decision.</p></div></details>

## It's working if

- Dense product and data views remain readable at their smallest delivered size.
- Newsreader carries paragraphs while the display and mono faces keep bounded roles.
- Physical colors are approved from measured material samples.
- Video texture, titles, captions, and color transforms survive the encoded-file review.

## Where it fits

Gruvbox Branding is one of six user-invoked themes. It chooses the visual direction and calls the model-invoked branding system. Press handles approved markdown rendering. Selecting Gruvbox alone also links the common engine without installing unrelated themes.
