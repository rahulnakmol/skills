# Group: Writing

Writing skills help people write better — and hold agents to the same standard. The group is a charter in this release: no skills have shipped yet. Planned work includes editorial review of ADRs and technical documents, documentation-quality linting, and enforcement of the explainer voice this repository already applies to itself through its authoring rules. The goal is the same as everywhere else in the bundle: the agent drafts and checks; the person owns what the words commit to.
