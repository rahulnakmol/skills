# Skill: Discover

**Group:** pm · **Invocation:** user-invoked · **Source:** [SKILL.md](https://github.com/tqnonline/skills/blob/main/skills/pm/discover/SKILL.md)

Discover turns a raw business problem — a meeting note, a transcript, a vague opportunity — into a structured analysis: stakeholders, root causes, an initiative classification, and success criteria. It is the entry point to the pm pipeline, the Framing gate's first input, and the point where the substrate and the hat are both established.

## When to invoke

- A new business problem, opportunity, or transformation candidate needs structured investigation.
- The user arrives with raw notes rather than a clean brief.

## How it fits

Discover opens by verifying or bootstrapping the initiative repository, then classifies which hat the engagement wears — product or transformation — before any clarifying question is asked. Clarification runs across five dimensions in the grill's themed rounds rather than a fixed form. It hands off to `map`.

## Key references

- [METHOD.md](https://github.com/tqnonline/skills/blob/main/skills/pm/discover/METHOD.md) covers the five dimensions, root-cause analysis (Five Whys, Fishbone), and the initiative-classification decision tree.
- [INITIATIVE-REPO.md](https://github.com/tqnonline/skills/blob/main/skills/pm/INITIATIVE-REPO.md) and [HATS.md](https://github.com/tqnonline/skills/blob/main/skills/pm/HATS.md) govern substrate and hat classification.

## How to use

Run `discover` with whatever you have — notes, a transcript, a rough problem statement. The skill establishes the substrate, classifies the hat, then questions across the five dimensions in themed grill rounds, capturing every answer immediately. Bring raw data directly; it is accepted and normalized rather than re-asked for.

## Best practices

- Do not skip straight to a solution — "we need to build X" gets redirected back to "what problem does X solve?"
- Triangulate across at least three stakeholder sources before writing the analysis down.
- Stop clarifying after four rounds without synthesis and analyze what is in hand.
