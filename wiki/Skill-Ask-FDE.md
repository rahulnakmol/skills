# Skill: Ask FDE

**Group:** developer · **Invocation:** user-invoked · **Source:** [SKILL.md](https://github.com/rahulnakmol/skills/blob/main/skills/developer/ask-fde/SKILL.md)

A router, not a specialist. When the user knows what they want but not which skill does it, `ask-fde` classifies the intent — inception, backlog, build, design, security, release, quality, production, models, governance, or branding — and calls the right skill directly.

## When to invoke

- The user asks "which skill do I need," or describes a goal without naming a skill

## How it fits

`ask-fde` doesn't belong to any single stage of the [role journey](Architecture-Role-Journey) — it's the front door across all of them, named after the forward-deployed-engineer persona: the one who knows the whole map and can point at the right door immediately.

## Sibling skills

Routes to any skill in the developer or branding group — see the full map in [SKILL.md](https://github.com/rahulnakmol/skills/blob/main/skills/developer/ask-fde/SKILL.md#procedure).
