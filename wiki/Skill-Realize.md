# Skill: Realize

**Group:** pm · **Invocation:** user-invoked · **Source:** [SKILL.md](https://github.com/tqnonline/skills/blob/main/skills/pm/realize/SKILL.md)

Realize tracks whether an initiative actually delivered what its case said it would: a benefits register traced to the case and the PRD's success metrics, leading indicators, actuals against projection, and a rollup to the practice's north star — efficiency and productivity as transformation value.

## When to invoke

- An initiative is live and its benefits need tracking against projection.
- The user asks for a benefits update, a north-star rollup, or a PI review.

## How it fits

Realize closes the loop the gates open: `case` projects, `realize` measures. When actuals diverge significantly from projection, the variance re-enters `discover` or `carve` as new work with its own case — the same discipline that opened the initiative, now applied to the gap it left.

## Key references

- [BENEFITS.md](https://github.com/tqnonline/skills/blob/main/skills/pm/realize/BENEFITS.md) covers the benefits register, leading indicators, the north star, and the variance loop-back.

## How to use

Run Realize once an initiative is delivering. Enter every case-projected benefit with a leading indicator, record actuals as they arrive, and roll each benefit up to the north star. Where a variance is significant, open it as new work rather than noting it and moving on.

## Best practices

- Every benefit needs at least one leading indicator — waiting for the lagging outcome finds problems too late.
- An initiative that cannot state its north-star contribution is a question for the grill, not a pass.
- Loop a significant variance back into `discover` or `carve` with its own case, not a footnote.
