# Skill: Architect

**Group:** developer · **Invocation:** mixed-invoked specialist · **Source:** [SKILL.md](https://github.com/rahulnakmol/skills/blob/main/skills/developer/architect/SKILL.md)

The design-gate charter. Leads cross-cutting technical design and produces ADR-ready decision packs with NFRs and interface contracts; the executable detail lives in its adapter, not in the charter itself.

## When to invoke

- An SDLC gate requires architect evidence
- A work item's pod charter names this role

## How it fits

`architect` is the Design stage of the [role journey](Architecture-Role-Journey). It consumes [recon](Skill-Recon)'s brief on brownfield estates, leads the Engineering Constraints, Components, and technical Trade-offs sections of the SPEC-TS ledger, and escalates to `responsible-ai-governance` the moment the work touches a regulated or consequential-decision context.
