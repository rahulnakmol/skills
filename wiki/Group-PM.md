# Group: PM — the AI-native transformation practice

The pm group is the business side of the same AI-native transformation coin as the developer group. Its 16 skills carry a product or program manager from a raw business problem to a delivered, measured benefit, with the goal, harness-tested, of making the PM a UX-driven, outcome-driven, efficient agentic owner of problem-solving — an agent fleet covering the PM's blind spots, and the PM assessing the fleet's in return.

Four human gates — Framing, Investment, Quality, Commitment — mirror the developer group's four, each opening with a blind-spot review before the human decides; see [GATES.md](https://github.com/tqnonline/skills/blob/main/skills/pm/GATES.md). PM work lives in an initiative repository — one per initiative by default, a monorepo of `initiatives/<slug>/` folders with Git LFS for binary artifacts, or the software repository's own `specs/` tree when the initiative delivers code; see [INITIATIVE-REPO.md](https://github.com/tqnonline/skills/blob/main/skills/pm/INITIATIVE-REPO.md). The seam to the developer group is real, not aspirational: a PM epic PRD may feed `impact` for engineering inception, and the delivery pipeline's `slice` and `raise` now carry Risk and Impediment item types plus an Azure DevOps tracker for exactly this handoff.

Two hats, one at a time — product or transformation, never both — cover the same range of business and functional work; see [HATS.md](https://github.com/tqnonline/skills/blob/main/skills/pm/HATS.md). Every artifact that reaches a gate has been through the grill — plain, with-docs, or provoke — the same interrogation discipline the developer group's `impact/GRILL.md` applies to engineering ideas, reused by reference rather than reinvented.
