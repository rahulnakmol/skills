# Group: Branding

Branding skills apply an identity — a company's or a person's — to everything an agent produces: tone of voice, color and palette, and the storytelling that turns an artifact into something an audience acts on. The group's charter is broad by design; its first shipped skill covers the delivery pipeline's most immediate need.

- [Press](Skill-Press) turns a signed PRD into a business-ready document and presentation — the first of the storytelling skills. It uses `PALETTE.md`'s default palette, or your own branding tokens when they are installed, and never mutates the PRD source.

A project or a person supplies their own branding tokens through palette overrides; the default exists so an export always works, not as a recommendation. Planned work extends the group beyond documents: presentation narratives, brand-consistent collateral, and tone-of-voice enforcement across everything the other groups produce.
