# Skill: TOM Architect

**Group:** pm · **Invocation:** user-invoked · **Source:** [SKILL.md](https://github.com/tqnonline/skills/blob/main/skills/pm/tom-architect/SKILL.md)

TOM Architect translates a business transformation into a structured Target Operating Model: process decomposition from L1 through L4, maturity assessment, organization and RACI design, a KPI framework, an AI augmentation overlay, and capability mapping to one of six enterprise platform stacks.

## When to invoke

- The transformation hat is active and a TOM is mandatory for this initiative.
- The user asks for process decomposition, maturity assessment, or platform capability mapping.

## How it fits

TOM Architect runs after `map` under the transformation hat, before `carve` extracts epics from the maturity gaps it surfaces. It commissions research where the current-state landscape is thin.

## Key references

- [TOM-METHOD.md](https://github.com/tqnonline/skills/blob/main/skills/pm/tom-architect/TOM-METHOD.md) covers process decomposition, the five-point maturity scale, organization and RACI, the KPI framework, the AI augmentation overlay, and the six platform stacks (Microsoft, SAP, Oracle, Salesforce, Workday, ServiceNow).
- [RESEARCH-AGENTS.md](https://github.com/tqnonline/skills/blob/main/skills/pm/RESEARCH-AGENTS.md) governs the evidence commissioned for maturity and platform-fit questions.

## How to use

Run TOM Architect once the transformation hat is confirmed. It works seven sequential phases — Discover, Scope, Analyze, Design, Map, Visualize, Deliver — and offers a review checkpoint before each artifact ships.

## Best practices

- Establish the current-state maturity baseline before designing the target — a target with no baseline is unrealistic.
- Do not scope all six TOM layers by default; ask which actually apply.
- Bake the AI augmentation overlay in from the first design pass, not as a phase-two addition.
