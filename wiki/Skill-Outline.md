# Outline

User-invoked structure stage of the writing sequence — use it after freewriting to assemble raw fragments into an ordered sequence of beats, grounding every term before a beat leans on it and cutting material that serves no beat.

Full documentation: https://tqnonline.github.io/skills/outline/
