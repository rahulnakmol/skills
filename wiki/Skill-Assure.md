# Skill: Assure

**Group:** developer · **Invocation:** mixed-invoked specialist · **Source:** [SKILL.md](https://github.com/rahulnakmol/skills/blob/main/skills/developer/assure/SKILL.md)

Quality and maintainability charter. Verifies the SPEC-TS ledger is actually testable and complete — requirements prioritized, constraints quantified, trade-offs backed by evidence — and produces a quality report with test gaps and tech-debt items destined for the backlog.

## When to invoke

- An SDLC gate requires assure evidence
- A work item's pod charter names this role

## How it fits

`assure` is the Maintainability stage of the [role journey](Architecture-Role-Journey). Its findings don't stop at a report — they re-enter [Slice](Skill-Slice) as continuous-improvement items, closing the loop the operability lane exists to keep open.
