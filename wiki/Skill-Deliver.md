# Deliver

Model-invoked charter for the delivery lane — continuous integration and delivery, supply chain, and release readiness. Use at the delivery gate to check a repository against the readiness checklist, ship a multi-concern change as a stack of dependency-ordered pull requests, resolve a merge or rebase conflict by intent, and record release evidence.
