# Responsible AI Governance

Responsible-AI and regulated-industry governance overlay. Use when work touches a regulated industry (financial services, healthcare, public sector) or an AI system that makes or influences consequential decisions, or when the user asks about audit, explainability, model risk, residency, NCUA, OSFI, SR 11-7, BCBS 239, NIST AI RMF, ISO 42001, or the EU AI Act.
