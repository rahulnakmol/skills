# Skill: Report

**Group:** pm · **Invocation:** user-invoked · **Source:** [SKILL.md](https://github.com/tqnonline/skills/blob/main/skills/pm/report/SKILL.md)

Report produces the leadership pack at weekly, fortnightly, quarterly, half-yearly, or yearly cadence: value first, then the 4Ps — Progress, Problems, Priorities, Perspective — sourced from the registers and the live delivery pipeline, with one compressing figure carrying the cadence's key decision.

## When to invoke

- A reporting cadence is due.
- The user asks for a status update, a steering pack, or a leadership update.

## How it fits

Report is the pm group's synthesis point: value and the north-star rollup from `realize`, Problems from `raid`, Priorities from `roadmap`, the cost and benefit baseline from `case`, and live pickup-protocol and pull-request state from the delivery pipeline. It renders through the branding group's `press`.

## Key references

- [REPORT-METHOD.md](https://github.com/tqnonline/skills/blob/main/skills/pm/report/REPORT-METHOD.md) covers the leadership pack, the 4Ps, value-first ordering, sources, and the five cadences.
- [VISUALS.md](https://github.com/tqnonline/skills/blob/main/skills/pm/VISUALS.md) governs the compressing figure every pack ships with.

## How to use

Run Report at the cadence that is due. It pulls from the registers automatically, writes the pack value-first in the 4Ps structure, and renders through `press` — the Perspective section is drafted by an agent at most, never owned by one.

## Best practices

- Open with value, not activity — a reader who stops after the first section should already know whether the initiative deserves continued attention.
- Never ship a pack with no compressing figure.
- Match the pack's altitude to its cadence — a weekly pack at yearly altitude buries the reader in noise.
