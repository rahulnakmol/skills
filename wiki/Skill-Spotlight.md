# Spotlight

Turns established context into the smallest source-grounded visual that helps a human understand or decide. Use when asked to visualize a flow, structure, relationship, comparison, or proposed change. Where the tool renders charts or diagrams inline, render through it; this skill chooses the one view and keeps the sources beside it.
